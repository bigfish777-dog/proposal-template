What we'd actually recommend

A few things we'd push back on in your brief, because we'd rather tell you our view than nod along:

LinkedIn as a primary channel.

You mentioned Meta, LinkedIn and Facebook. For a reformer equipment supplier selling primarily to studio owners and gym operators, LinkedIn has a role in B2B outreach but it's not where the buying conversation happens. We'd recommend deprioritising LinkedIn ads in phase one and revisiting it in month six once we've got data on where the demand actually comes from. The budget you'd put into LinkedIn is better spent on Google Ads (where buyer intent is highest) and Meta (where the visual story of a reformer studio sells itself).

PPC from day one.

Day-one PPC works when you've got a converting offer, clear tracking, and inventory ready to ship. We'll build the accounts and run them from launch, but expect the first 60 days post-launch to be learning data, not profitable spend. Realistic ROAS thinking starts in month three.

Content strategy versus content execution.

You asked for "content strategy and overall brand positioning." We'll deliver the strategy in phase one. Execution of weekly content is part of the post-launch retainer, not the build phase. Worth being clear so expectations align.

Social media management.

Once LnR is live we'll run content production and posting on Meta and LinkedIn as part of the retainer. We're being deliberately clear: this is a partnership where we manage the channels and report on them, not a daily community management service. If you want someone replying to DMs within 30 minutes, that's a different scope and should sit with someone in-house.

The 90-day build (Phase one)

Month one: foundations

  • Discovery, brand positioning workshop, messaging strategy
  • Site architecture, wireframing, ecommerce platform decision (likely Shopify given UK/Irish multi-currency requirements)
  • Keyword strategy and SEO/AEO foundation document
  • Content strategy document delivered
  • Ad account builds (Google, Meta), pixel and CAPI implementation, tracking setup

Month two: build

  • Website design (premium, educational, brand-aligned)
  • Ecommerce build with multi-currency and quote-request flow
  • On-page SEO implementation
  • Email marketing platform setup, welcome sequence, transactional automations
  • Initial ad creative production for Google and Meta
  • Analytics dashboard build

Month three: launch

  • Final testing, QA, content population
  • Soft launch and bug-fix window
  • Public launch with paid traffic live from day one
  • Reporting infrastructure live

The space-planning tool

A custom tool where studio and gym owners input their room dimensions and the site generates a layout showing how many reformers the space can comfortably fit.

We're treating this as a separate workstream because it's a software build, not a marketing deliverable. It has its own discovery, scoping, testing, and revision cycles. Running it as a separate track means if the dev work slips, it doesn't hold up the LnR launch.

What the fee covers:

  • Discovery and technical scoping
  • UX and interface design
  • Build (we'll use a combination of subcontracted dev and AI-assisted tooling for efficiency)
  • Testing across browsers and devices
  • Launch and handover documentation

Ongoing maintenance from month four onwards falls under the post-launch retainer.

What we're not doing

Being explicit so there's no confusion:

  • We're not running daily community management or DM response on social. Channels are managed and posted; conversations stay with you.
  • We're not handling customer service, order fulfilment, or post-purchase support for ecommerce orders.
  • We're not building a fully custom ecommerce platform. We're building on Shopify (or similar) because it's the right tool for the job and won't slow us down.
  • We're not running organic LinkedIn content as a primary channel in phase one (see "what we'd recommend" above).
  • We're not handling PR, partnerships, or influencer outreach. If you want that later, we'll scope it as an add-on.
